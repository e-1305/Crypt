# Review ritual: Round of 10s

Grade the whole product (live site, CLI, docs, video script) as 10 personas.

## Personas
1. Kiroween judge (Kiro TAM): specs, steering, hooks, "Kiro surprised you".
2. Kiro Ambassador (simple, focused, clean UI/UX, CI/CD).
3. Kiro Ambassador (technological implementation + presentation, breadth of Kiro surfaces).
4. CISO.
5. Vulnerability-management lead.
6. SOC analyst.
7. AppSec engineer.
8. Engineering manager.
9. Open-source maintainer.
10. VC.

Each persona gives:
- a grade from 1 to 10,
- one sentence on why,
- the single fix that would add the most points.

## Official criteria
Then score each of the five official criteria from 1 to 10: Potential Impact, Technological Implementation, Design, Innovation, Presentation.

## Output
1. A table: persona, grade, why, top fix. Then the five criteria scores.
2. A ranked fix list, ordered by value per hour (points gained per hour of work).

## Rules
- Cite a file, page or line for every critique.
- No invented users or numbers.
- Numbers come only from docs/FACTS.md.
