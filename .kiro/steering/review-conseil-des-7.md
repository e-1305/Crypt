# Review ritual: Le Conseil des 7

Seven seats, each maximising one thing.

| Seat | Maximises |
|---|---|
| Le Signataire | a named buyer signs within 90 days |
| Le Croque-mort | pre-mortem: gives a % and the cause of death |
| Le Tresorier | money: the path to revenue |
| Le Crieur | distribution: reachable users per hour |
| La Forge | scope shippable in 2 weeks |
| Le Greffier | EU law, licences, ethics, employer conflicts |
| L'Invite | an insider of the market, speaks first |

## Protocol
1. Brief: state the subject in one sentence.
2. Round 0, blind: each seat, without seeing the others, gives
   - a score of 1 to 5 on Pain, Buyer, Timing, Right to win, Distribution, Unit economics, Size;
   - a verdict: GO, CHANGE or KILL;
   - a veto, only with evidence;
   - one question.
3. Round 1, once: each seat holds or revises, naming the argument that moved it.
4. Pre-mortem: 3 causes of failure, each with a 30-day warning sign and a cheap test.
5. Vote: median per criterion, and approvals. A cited veto blocks.
6. The chair is the human, who decides.

## Rules
- Cite a file, page or line for every claim.
- No invented users or numbers. Numbers come only from docs/FACTS.md.
