# Real-human playtest kit

Three people, two minutes each, anonymous. The goal is to back the persona review in [PLAYTEST_LOG.md](../PLAYTEST_LOG.md) with real humans from outside the project (classmates, friends). Results go in [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md).

## Before you start

- Pick people who have never seen CRYPT and are not on the team.
- Open the site on a laptop, on the landing page. Do not demo it.
- Start a timer on your phone. Keep one note sheet per person (below).
- Do not explain, hint, or defend. If they ask a question, say "what would you try?"

## Consent line

Read this first: "I take notes while you use it. The notes are anonymous: no names, and please do not show me anything from your employer. You can stop at any time."

Write no names and no employer data on the sheet. Use P1, P2, P3.

## Script (read aloud, 5 lines)

1. "Thanks for helping. This takes about two minutes."
2. "I am testing the site, not you. You cannot get it wrong."
3. "Please think out loud, and say what you are looking at and what you expect."
4. "I will not answer questions until the end, so do what you would do alone."
5. "Here is a laptop. I will give you four small tasks, one at a time."

Do not say what CRYPT is, what it does, or what to click.

## Tasks

Read each task aloud, then stay quiet.

1. After 10 seconds on the page, say in one sentence what CRYPT does. Write down the sentence word for word.
2. Find the proof that a fix really happened.
3. Scan a repo of your choice. Note the time from "go" to the first scan result.
4. Say whether you would install it at work, and if not, why not.

## Note sheet (one per person)

```
Person: P__   Date: ____   Role (student / dev / other): ____

1. First sentence (after 10 s, verbatim):

2. Where they got stuck (page, element, how long):

3. Time to first scan (mm:ss, or "did not finish"):

4. Found the proof of a fix? (yes / no, where they looked first):

5. Would share (yes / no):

6. Would install at work (yes / no, and why not):

7. Grade (/10):
```

## After the three sessions

Copy each sheet into one row of [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md). Keep quotes short and anonymous. Report the numbers as they are, including bad ones. Do not change any score because the team fixed the finding.
